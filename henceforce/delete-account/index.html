Delete your Henceforce account

Henceforce allows you to permanently delete your account and the cloud data associated with it.

How to delete your account

  1. Open Henceforce and sign in to your account.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Confirm that you want to permanently delete the account.

Data that is deleted

When account deletion is completed, the following data is deleted:

  • Your Henceforce account and account identifier.
  • Your email address associated with the account.
  • Task data and other content synced to Henceforce cloud services.

Data stored on your device

Data stored only on your device is not necessarily removed when your online account is deleted. You can remove local app data by clearing Henceforce's app data in Android settings or by uninstalling the app.

Retention

Henceforce does not intentionally retain deleted account data after account deletion is completed, except where retention is required by law.

Need help? If you cannot access the in-app account deletion option, contact play-review@suwonmars.com.
